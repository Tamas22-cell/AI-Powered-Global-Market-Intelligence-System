# AI-Powered Global Market Intelligence System

AI-powered financial market intelligence project built with Python and Discord automation.

The system combines macroeconomic data, traditional markets, crypto derivatives, on-chain liquidity and automated market monitoring into one research workflow.

## Core Modules

### 1. Macroeconomics & TradFi
- Interest rates
- Inflation
- Economic indicators
- Equity and bond market risk signals
- Broader market regime analysis

### 2. Crypto Derivatives
- Funding rates
- Open interest
- BTC and ETH positioning
- Leverage conditions
- Liquidation risk

### 3. On-Chain Intelligence
- Stablecoin flows
- Exchange balances
- Capital movement
- Blockchain-based liquidity signals
- Market risk conditions

### 4. Automated Market Intelligence
- Python-based analysis
- Discord integration
- Automated monitoring
- Market alerts
- Decision-support workflow

## Tech Stack

- Python
- Discord Bot
- APIs
- Financial market data
- Blockchain / On-chain analytics
- Crypto derivatives data
- Macroeconomic data

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to combine multiple market data sources into a single intelligence system that can identify liquidity conditions, market regimes, risk signals and potential opportunities across traditional and crypto markets.

Active development and research project.
